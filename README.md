# Hospitality-Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

The Hospitality Analytics Project is a comprehensive data analytics solution designed to help hotel businesses monitor performance, optimize revenue, improve occupancy rates, and reduce booking cancellations. This project leverages Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Tableau to transform raw hospitality data into actionable business insights. Based on booking, revenue, room utilization, and customer behavior data, the dashboard enables stakeholders to make informed, data-driven decisions.

----

## 🎯 Project Objectives

• Analyze hotel booking and revenue data across multiple cities and properties.
• Measure key hospitality KPIs such as Revenue, Occupancy Rate, Booking Volume, and Cancellation Rate.
• Identify top-performing hotels, cities, and room categories based on revenue contribution.
• Evaluate room utilization and booking patterns to improve capacity planning.
• Compare weekday and weekend performance to understand customer booking behavior.
• Track weekly and monthly revenue trends to identify seasonality and demand fluctuations.
• Analyze booking cancellations and no-show rates to minimize revenue loss.
• Provide data-driven recommendations to increase profitability and operational efficiency.

## 📌 Dashboard Insights

The analysis revealed several important business insights:

Revenue Analysis
 • Total Revenue: ₹1.70 Billion
 • Mumbai generated the highest revenue among all cities.
 • Luxury category hotels contributed significantly more revenue than business hotels.

Occupancy & Bookings
 • Occupancy Rate: 57.87%
 • Total Bookings: 134,590
 • Utilized Capacity: 57%

Cancellation Analysis
 • Cancellation Rate: 24.83%
 • A significant portion of bookings were cancelled, impacting realized revenue.

Seasonal Trends
 • May emerged as the strongest booking month.
 • Week 29 recorded the highest revenue performance.
 • June experienced comparatively lower booking activity.

Hotel Performance
 • Atliq Exotica recorded the highest revenue among hotel properties.
 • Luxury room categories consistently outperformed business categories across all cities.

## 📋 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business recommendations were identified:

Reduce Cancellation Rates
 • Introduce advance payment requirements.
 • Design flexible cancellation policies based on customer segments.

Increase Revenue Growth
 • Focus marketing efforts on high-performing cities.
 • Promote premium services and room upgrades.

Improve Capacity Utilization
 • Expand room inventory where occupancy remains consistently high.
 • Implement demand forecasting strategies.

Strengthen Low-Performing Areas
 • Investigate properties with lower occupancy or visibility.
 • Enhance customer acquisition campaigns through booking platforms.
